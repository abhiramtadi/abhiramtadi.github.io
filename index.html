PROJECT 01

Classic SD multi-tool

REVERSE ENGINEERING · SOLIDWORKS · 16 PARTS · GA + 4 DETAIL DRAWINGS TO BS 8888 · 3D PRINTED PROTOTYPE

A 58 mm multi-tool rebuilt from the physical product. No manufacturer CAD existed, so every part was measured with digital calipers and modelled from scratch, down to the stamped depressions on the 0.5 mm liners and the crosshatch on the nail file.

The finished assembly holds both mechanism states, with the back springs built as a free-shape master and a driven seated configuration. It is documented with a general assembly, BOM and four detail drawings, and printed as a physical fit-and-motion prototype.
