About

I am a product design engineer based in London. I finished an MSc in Advanced Product Design Engineering and Manufacturing at Kingston University London in 2025, following a BTech in Mechanical Engineering at SRM University in Chennai, and I passed the Certified SolidWorks Associate exam in July 2026.

Most of my practical experience comes from building things in SolidWorks: single parts, multi part assemblies, and the drawings that go with them. The one I would point at first is a reverse engineering build where I took a 58 mm multi-tool apart, measured every piece with digital calipers, and rebuilt the whole thing as a finished parametric assembly on one shared datum scheme.

Alongside CAD I have worked with ANSYS for CFD, and in the Kingston labs with 3D laser scanning and metrology equipment. I am looking for a junior CAD technician or design engineer role where I can keep doing this full time.
