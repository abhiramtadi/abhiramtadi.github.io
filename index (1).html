Reverse Engineering & CAD Portfolio Project

VICTORINOX CLASSIC SD — 2026

Self-directed · SolidWorks, digital vernier calipers

Loading model…
Drag to rotate · Scroll to zoom · Right-drag to pan

Reverse-engineering a 58 mm, seven-function Victorinox Classic SD multi-tool from the physical product into a fully parametric SolidWorks model — no manufacturer CAD or published drawings were available, so every part was measured and modelled from scratch.

  • Hybrid measurement strategy: digital vernier calipers for functional dimensions (pivot diameters, hole centres, layer thicknesses), cross-checked against the 58 × 18 × 9 mm overall envelope, with photographic sketch-tracing for free-form tool profiles.
  • Common datum scheme (origin at the rear pivot centre, X-axis through both pin centres) keeps the layer stack-up and assembly mates consistent across all parts.
  • Manufacturing detail modelled as real geometry rather than appearance maps — stamped depressions with draft and asymmetric blend fillets on the 0.5 mm liners, and a wrapped crosshatch abrasive texture on the nail file.
DONE: liners, handle scales, nail file/screwdriver, back springs, blade
NEXT: scissors, final pivot mates, interference/collision detection, mass properties, four BS-standard drawings

The back springs are preloaded in situ, so once the assembly is complete the plan is to model two mechanism states: a closed state (tool folded, springs seated/assembled) and an open state (blade deployed, springs in their free, as-manufactured form) — with the free state as the master part and the seated shape captured as a driven configuration.
